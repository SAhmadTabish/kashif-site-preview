How it happened

  1. 2020

    A phone and no audience

    He starts making videos on his own. Nobody is watching yet. He posts anyway.

  2. 2023

    All in on YouTube

    He commits to a schedule and works at it every day. The numbers stay small. He changes the format instead of quitting.

  3. 2024

    TikTok

    The Gujjar trend and movie dialogues take over Pakistani TikTok. Kashi does them his way. The videos blow up, and soon other creators are lip-syncing his audio.

  4. Then

    Adalat

    A second account, Mr. Malik Kashi 2, for videos over a minute long. Slow at first. Then the courtroom videos hit: 20 to 25 million views on single episodes, and the account passes two million followers.

  5. Now

    Seven people and longer films

    Three-minute comedies, short films like The Last Door, vlogs and the characters. Seven people now make the videos with him.

The cast

Kashi made every video alone for the first years. These seven stayed through the slow stretch and are in nearly every video now.

Questions

Where is Kashi from?

Basti Abdul Kareem, a village near Chanigoth in Bahawalpur District, Punjab. He was born there in 2002, grew up there, and still films there.

What did he study?

Computer science. He has a BS from The Islamia University of Bahawalpur.

How can a brand work with him?

Message him on TikTok or Facebook. Brands, collaborations and appearances all go through there.

Which account should I follow?

Mr. Malik Kashi 2 on TikTok, for the longer comedy and the courtroom series. Mr. Malik Kashi is the original account, where the Gujjar-trend videos first went viral. He is also on Facebook as Mr Kashi and on YouTube as Mr. Kashi Fun.

What is Adalat?

His courtroom comedy series on Mr. Malik Kashi 2. Single episodes have reached 20 to 25 million views.

Does he make longer videos too?

Yes. Three-minute comedies, vlogs, the character videos, and short films like The Last Door, a psychological thriller series on YouTube.

Who edits his videos?

Kashi does most of the editing himself.

What does he do off camera?

He plays cricket, whenever there is time.

Work with Kashi.

Brands, collaborations, appearances: message him on TikTok or Facebook.
